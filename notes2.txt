Bock (1970, 1972), wright \& Mead (1977) and Yen (1981) partition persons into N_{grp} groups

$$
Q_{1i}=\sum_{g=1}^{N_{grp}}
\frac
{\left(\sum_{v\in g}(X_{vi}-E(X_{vi})\right)^2}
{\sum_{v\in g}V(X_{vi})}
$$

The item chi-square in RUMM \cite{rumm} is a version of this grouping persons based on estimated person location, disregarding those with extreme scores. The infit test statistic in winsteps \cite{winsteps} is a version of this with one person in each group.

Wright \& Panchapakesan (1969) partition persons into groups defined by the total score and calculate
$$
Q_{2i}=\sum_s\sum_{v\in s}
$$

the outfit in winsteps is
$$
OUTFIT_i=\frac1n Q_{2i}
$$
