For ease of exposition, the main text restricts attention to dichotomous items, $X_{vi}\in\{0,1\}$. Here we give the corresponding formulas for the general case of ordinal items with $M+1$ response categories, $X_{vi}\in\{0,1,\ldots,M\}$ (assuming, for ease of notation, that all items have the same number of response categories).

The parametric form of the response probabilities is
$$
P(X_{vi}=x|\theta)=\frac{\exp(\sum_{h=1}^x(\theta-\beta_{ih}))}{1+\sum_{z=1}^M\exp(\sum_{h=1}^z(\theta-\beta_{iz})},
$$
which reduces to the dichotomous form used in the main text when $M=1$ and $\beta_{i1}=\delta_i$.

Everything in the sections \ref{sec:fit-stat} and \ref{sec:also-fit-stat} work in exactly the same way for this more general model.
