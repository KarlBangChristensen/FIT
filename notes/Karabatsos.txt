The faulty statistical properties of the residual fit statistics
do not allow either a convenient or a straightforward approach to Rasch fit
analysis. For instance, given a residual fit statistic, the use of a single
minimum critical value for misfit diagnosis across different testing situations, 
where the situations vary in sample and test properties, leads to both the
overdetection and underdetection of misfit.


Karabatsos G. A critique of Rasch residual fit statistics. J Appl Meas. 2000;1(2):152-76.
PMID: 12029176
