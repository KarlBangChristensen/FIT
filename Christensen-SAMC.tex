The Rasch models, with their assumption of local independence of responses, are
a formal representation and deﬁnition of measurement. Hence, an analysis according
to a Rasch model will reveal a violation of this assumption as an anomaly [AND 04].
Andrich describes two approaches that can be taken when there is a data-model misﬁt,
as is the case with dependence in the data. In the traditional approach, a model that
better ﬁts the data is sought whenever there is a misﬁt. In the Rasch approach, misﬁt
reveals anomalies. Ideally, items are modiﬁed and new data without the anomalies are
collected so that measurements conform better to the measurement model and also
enhance the validity of the assessment.
